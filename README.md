# Open-HR: The All-in-One OS for Global Teams

**Open-HR is a free, open-source, and AI-powered HR and payroll platform designed for the way the world works today.** We're building a unified solution to manage global teams, combining the best features of industry leaders like Deel, Rippling, ADP, Remote, Papaya Global, Gusto, and Oyster HR into a single, powerful platform.

## Why Open-HR?

Managing a global workforce is complex. Companies often have to stitch together multiple tools for payroll, compliance, benefits, and HR, leading to data silos, inefficiencies, and high costs. Open-HR aims to solve this by providing a single source of truth for your entire team, no matter where they are in the world.

- **Unified Platform:** One place to manage everything from hiring and onboarding to payroll and offboarding.
- **AI-Powered:** Leverage the latest in AI to automate tasks, gain insights, and make smarter decisions.
- **Global First:** Built for global teams, with support for multiple currencies, languages, and local compliance.
- **Open Source:** Free to use, community-driven, and customizable to your needs.
- **Secure & Compliant:** Built with security and compliance at its core, so you can rest easy.

## Core Features

Open-HR is designed to be a comprehensive solution for managing your global team. Here are some of the core features we're building:

| Feature | Description | Inspired By |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Global Payroll** | Run payroll in multiple currencies, with automated tax calculations and compliance. | Deel, Papaya Global |
| **Benefits Administration** | Manage health insurance, retirement plans, and other benefits for your global team. | Gusto, Rippling |
| **Hiring & Onboarding** | Streamline your hiring process, from creating job posts to onboarding new hires. | Oyster HR, Remote |
| **Time & Attendance** | Track hours, manage time off, and ensure compliance with local labor laws. | ADP, Rippling |
| **Employee Management** | A single place for all employee data, documents, and performance management. | Rippling, Gusto |
| **AI-Powered HR** | Get insights into your team, automate repetitive tasks, and get AI-assisted support. | (State of the Art) |
| **Compliance & Security** | Stay compliant with local laws and keep your data secure with enterprise-grade security. | All |

## Tech Stack

We're building Open-HR on a modern, scalable, and secure tech stack:

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, NestJS, and PostgreSQL
- **AI/ML:** Python, TensorFlow, and PyTorch
- **Infrastructure:** Docker, Kubernetes, and AWS/GCP/Azure

## Getting Started

We're still in the early stages of development, but we're excited to have you join us! Here's how you can get started:

1. **Fork the repository:** `git clone https://github.com/your-username/open-hr.git`
2. **Install dependencies:** `npm install`
3. **Run the development server:** `npm run dev`
